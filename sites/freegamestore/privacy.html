Privacy Policy

How FreeGameStore handles player data, creator accounts and AI connector requests.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Playing games and browsing

Games are free and must not include advertising trackers, behavioral tracking or fingerprinting. Games may save progress and preferences locally on your device. Connected features, such as accounts, cloud saves and multiplayer, send the information needed to provide those features to our services. Clearing browser storage removes local progress and preferences.

Cloudflare hosts the storefront and services and processes network requests, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure them. The storefront can use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate, cookieless traffic measurements. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Creator accounts

Signing in uses GitHub identity and FreeGameStore session credentials. Creator services process account identifiers, game metadata, source files and deployment information to manage and publish your games. Authentication uses secure session cookies; these are separate from tracking cookies.

MCP connector

What it reads and writes, and why

When your assistant calls a tool, FreeGameStore processes the tool arguments and returns results to that assistant. Reference and catalog tools read public platform documentation, game metadata and source files. Authenticated tools can list your games, read or update your game files, provision and publish repositories, inspect deployments, and create, continue, read or delete your VibeCode build sessions. Sessions include prompts, conversation messages, generated code and build status. Administrative tools are restricted to platform administrators.

We use this information to carry out the actions you request, authenticate you, enforce ownership and investigate failures or abuse. Write attempts, dry runs and denied actions generate an audit log containing your GitHub login, timestamp, tool name, redacted arguments and result details. Secret redaction reduces exposure; avoid putting secrets or sensitive personal information into prompts or files.

Tokens and retention

The MCP OAuth provider stores authorization grants and token records in Cloudflare KV. The grant carries the FreeGameStore session credential used to authenticate downstream requests. MCP access tokens have a 30-day lifetime; the underlying FreeGameStore session is also checked, so an expired or revoked session requires sign-in again. Refresh tokens and authorization grants are managed by the OAuth provider; the service does not promise that all grant records disappear when an access token expires. The sign-in handoff state expires after 10 minutes.

MCP audit entries expire after 90 days. Game files and GitHub commit history remain until removed or managed in the repository; disconnecting does not erase published source. VibeCode sessions remain until deleted through the creator tools or account deletion. Provider and infrastructure logs may follow their own retention policies.

Who receives the data

  • Cloudflare hosts our Workers, KV authorization and audit records, Durable Object sessions and R2 game assets.
  • GitHub provides identity and stores game repositories, commits and deployment workflows. Published game source is public.
  • Your connected AI assistant receives tool results under its own privacy policy. If you call agent_build, the selected AI provider (such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or GitHub) receives the prompt, conversation and relevant code needed to build the game. Provider API keys are used for provider authentication; do not include them in public issues.

Your controls

Review the consent screen and requested actions before connecting. Inspect your own events with mcp_audit_log. Remove the connector in your assistant to stop its use. To invalidate existing FreeGameStore sessions, use Sign out, which affects other signed-in clients too. Disconnecting does not delete games, sessions or existing audit entries. Manage games and sessions in the creator console, or contact support about account deletion and privacy requests.

Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the maintainers through FreeGameStore support. Do not post private information in a public issue. Updates to this policy are published here with the revision date. See our terms for connector use.

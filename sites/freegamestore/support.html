FreeGameStore support

Help with games, creator accounts and the MCP connector.

Contact us

Open a support issue in our platform repository, or check existing issues. Include your client and version, the time, the tool name and the error message. Public issues are visible to everyone: remove tokens, API keys, private code and personal information.

MCP connection problems

  1. Use https://mcp.freegamestore.online/mcp with Streamable HTTP. Check your plan and workspace allow custom connectors.
  2. Remove and reconnect FreeGameStore in your assistant's connector settings. Complete sign-in and consent again. In Claude Code, use /mcp to authenticate.
  3. If tools are missing, refresh the connection and enable FreeGameStore in the current conversation. Start with platform_guide. A denied write may mean the game belongs to another account or the service is temporarily read-only.

Disconnect or revoke access

Remove FreeGameStore from your assistant's connector settings (Claude Code: claude mcp remove freegamestore). To invalidate existing FreeGameStore sessions, use Sign out; this also affects your other signed-in clients. Reconnect and sign in again when needed. Removing a connector does not delete published games, repository history or VibeCode sessions.

Manage your data

Use the creator console to manage games and sessions. The connector provides mcp_audit_log to inspect your recent write attempts. For deletion requests or privacy questions, contact the maintainers through the support issue link above without including sensitive data. See MCP privacy, terms and the connector guide.
